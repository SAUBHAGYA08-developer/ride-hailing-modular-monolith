2 · Rider — book a ride

A short trip lands on the 50 minimum fare, which is below every coupon minimum — use the long preset when you want to show a coupon actually applying.

Substitution never crosses a category family: a HATCHBACK may be upgraded to a SEDAN free of charge, but AUTO and BIKE are only ever served by their own category — no car is sent to an auto request and no auto to a bike request.

"Get estimate" prices the real trip above and books nothing — no driver is held and no coupon use is taken. It is also where you check a coupon: one that cannot apply comes back as a reason on the estimate rather than as an error, so there is no fare to type in by hand.

A rider holds PAYMENT_READ but not PAYMENT_COLLECT, so the retry answers 403 — shown in the banner below rather than thrown. The cancellation fee is not on the ride's payment field; it is a separate money line and only appears in this list.

3 · Driver — go online, move, serve

Completing a ride collects the fare, so the method is mandatory on POST /rides/{id}/complete — every Complete button on this page sends the one selected here. A declined payment is not an error: the ride still completes and the payment comes back FAILED, which is what "Retry payment" then re-collects, optionally on a different instrument. To force a decline, set payment.simulated.failure.methods to a CSV of methods in the Configurations tab of section 5 (NONE means all succeed).

A position lives in Redis for driver.location.ttl.seconds (60 by default). Once it expires the driver falls out of matching and bookings answer NO_DRIVER_IN_RADIUS — keep auto-ping on while demonstrating. BUSY is never set by hand: the ride module flips it atomically when it claims a driver, and clears it on complete or cancel.

4 · Matching map — seeded driver positions

The letter in each marker is the category — S sedan, H hatchback, A auto, B bike. Being inside the radius is not enough on its own: only a driver whose category may serve the request can be matched, and AUTO and BIKE serve nothing but themselves.

Distances are the same haversine the matcher uses. The dashed circle is ride.search.radius.km — load the configurations in section 5 to sync its real value.

Authorities are baked into the JWT at login, so an admin token minted before the DRIVER_FLEET_READ migration ran will answer 403 here — log the admin out and back in (section 1) and it works.

Bookable is the only honest answer to "how many drivers can take a ride right now": AVAILABLE, holding an active vehicle, and still reporting a live position in Redis. AVAILABLE alone is MySQL's reservation state — a driver whose position expired past driver.location.ttl.seconds stays AVAILABLE forever yet can never be matched. That gap is onDutyWithoutLiveLocation, and it is the number an operator otherwise only learns about from rider complaints.
